# Deposit Requester: worked examples

All names and amounts are made up.

## A percentage deposit

**Owner:** Rob accepted the $2,580 fence estimate. I want 20% down to hold the 14th. Zelle to 555-0142. I'm Alvarez Fence.

20% of $2,580 is $516.00. The balance is $2,064.00.

**Text message**
> Hi Rob, it's Alvarez Fence. Great, let's do it. To hold the 14th I need a $516 deposit. Zelle to 555-0142 works. The $2,064 balance is due when we finish.

**Email**
Subject: Holding the 14th for your fence

> Hi Rob,
>
> Glad you're going ahead. To hold the 14th, I need a $516 deposit, which is 20% of the $2,580. You can send it by Zelle to 555-0142. The $2,064 balance is due when we finish.
>
> Alvarez Fence

**Once it's paid**
> Got the $516, Rob. You're booked for the 14th. The $2,064 balance is due when we finish.

**Check before you send**
- You didn't give a cancellation policy, so I left it out. Tell me what it is and I'll add a line.

**When to send:** today, right after he said yes.

## The owner's own terms

**Owner:** Dana booked the photo shoot on the 22nd. I need $150 by Friday. Non-refundable, but I'll move the date once with 48 hours' notice. Venmo @brightframe. I'm Chris.

**Text message**
> Hi Dana, it's Chris. To hold the 22nd I need a $150 deposit by Friday. Venmo @brightframe. It's non-refundable, but I can move the date once if you give me 48 hours.
