# Follow-Up Writer: worked examples

All names and amounts are made up.

## Follow-up 1, easy check-in

**Owner:** I quoted Maria $3,200 for a new fence nine days ago and haven't heard back. I can hold the price through the 20th.

**Which one:** Follow-up 1, a little late. Easy check-in with the hold date as a gentle bonus.

**Text message**
> Hi Maria, it's [your name]. Wanted to check in on the $3,200 fence quote. Still thinking it over, or did plans change? Either answer is fine. I can hold the price through the 20th.

**Email**
Subject: Checking in on your fence quote

> Hi Maria,
>
> I wanted to check in on the quote for the new fence ($3,200). Still thinking it over, or did plans change? Either answer is totally fine.
>
> I can hold the price through the 20th. If you have questions about the materials or timing, just reply and I'll sort it out.
>
> Thanks,
> [your name]

**When to send:** Tuesday around 10am.

**What next:** If no reply, send follow-up 2 (a reason to reply) in about a week.

## Closing the loop

**Owner:** Third try on the Hendersons' gutter quote. Two follow-ups, no reply.

**Which one:** Follow-up 3, closing the loop. This is the last message.

**Text message**
> Hi, it's [your name]. I haven't heard back on the gutter quote, so I'm going to close it out on my end. If you ever want to pick it up again, just text me. No hard feelings either way.

**When to send:** Wednesday around 10am.

**What next:** That's the last one. Leave it alone for a month. If you want, send something new later, like a spring gutter-cleaning reminder.
