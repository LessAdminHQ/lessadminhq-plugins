# Pay Nudger: worked examples

All names and amounts are made up.

## Friendly, first reminder

**Owner:** Sarah M. owes me $850 for a bathroom faucet replacement. 12 days late. She pays by Venmo.

**Tone picked:** Friendly (12 days, first reminder). Say "make it firmer" to switch.

**Text message**
> Hi Sarah, it's [your name]. Just a quick reminder that the $850 for the bathroom faucet is still open. Probably slipped through the cracks! You can Venmo me at [handle] whenever you get a sec. Thanks!

**Email**
Subject: Quick reminder: $850 for the bathroom faucet

> Hi Sarah,
>
> Hope the new faucet's working well. Just a friendly reminder that the $850 for the replacement was due 12 days ago and is still open.
>
> The easiest way to pay is Venmo: [handle]. If something's off with the bill, just reply and let me know.
>
> Thanks,
> [your name]

**When to send:** Tomorrow (a weekday) around 10am.

**If they don't reply:** Wait 5 days, then send a Firm reminder that asks for a specific payment date.

## Batch

**Owner:** Here's who owes me: Sarah M. $850, 12 days. Dan R. $2,400, 45 days, promised twice. The Hendersons $320, 68 days.

Sorted most-overdue first:

1. **The Hendersons, $320, 68 days (Final).** Before I write this one: what will you actually do if they don't pay? Pause future work, offer a payment plan, or small claims?
2. **Dan R., $2,400, 45 days, broken promises (Final).** Same question. Your answer for the Hendersons can cover both.
3. **Sarah M., $850, 12 days (Friendly)**

**Text message (Sarah):**
> Hi Sarah, it's [your name]. Quick reminder that the $850 for the bathroom faucet is still open. Probably slipped through the cracks! [how to pay]. Thanks!

**When to send:** Monday 10am.

**This week's plan**
- Monday: Sarah (friendly)
- Tuesday: Dan and the Hendersons (final, once you tell me the next step)
- Friday: check who's paid; I'll write the follow-ups for anyone who hasn't
